# Blood Magic

Blood magic extracts creature blood, creates loyal companions and drains enemies to restore your health. All three books work in your hand or through the Arcane Wand, including from a carried Spellbook Pouch.

Carrying **Bloody Book** slowly drains health. The curse stops when you remove it; its pulses do not interrupt blood extraction.

## Leech Book

Hold **RMB** while aiming at a living creature or player within **16 blocks**. The blood stream locks onto that target. Each full second deals **30 base damage**, restores **20 health** and costs **10 mana**. Armor and damage protection apply; a rejected hit gives no healing. Healing stops at your maximum health.

Keep holding RMB: damage, healing and mana spending begin after the first full second, then repeat each second until the channel ends.

A glowing crimson tether and bright droplets flowing toward you show the siphon. Small pulses mark the focus and the target.

With Leech selected on the **Arcane Wand**, hold **RMB** standing or crouching. Carry the Leech Book loose or inside a carried Spellbook Pouch.

Release RMB, lose sight, move out of range or change your held item to stop. Death, an invalid target or insufficient mana also ends the channel. It does not switch to another target automatically. Player targets require **PvP** to be enabled.

Craft with exactly **one Rex Blood Sample, one Dreadmaw Blood Sample, one Yeti Blood Sample and one Emberwulf Blood Sample** at **Arcanist's Workbench → Spellblades**, **12 seconds**. Extract each with the Bloody Book from a different living creature. These samples can also be processed into DNA or used as Blood Furnace fuel.

## From blood to a companion

1. Wear the full **Arcane Veil set** to reduce creature detection to five blocks. Stay outside that range while preparing an extraction. The separate **Invisibility Cloak** conceals you from creatures and players.
2. Hold **Bloody Book**, aim at a living creature within **eight blocks**, and press **RMB** once. Keep aiming and holding the book for **eight uninterrupted seconds**. Taking damage, changing items, losing sight or moving out of range interrupts extraction. It costs no mana.
3. Collect the **Blood Droplet**. Its tooltip names the creature, and its identity stays with the vial through storage, trading and restarts. **Each individual gives blood only once**, shared across players. A full inventory leaves the vial on the ground.
4. Place a **Vitae Extractor** and use **Interact** to open its brewing screen. Select blood and **Essence of Life** from the inventory below: **LMB transfers a stack; RMB transfers one**. The essence well feeds three vial stations, with five reserve slots. Extraction begins automatically and shows the species and progress. After **five loaded seconds**, **one droplet + one essence = one DNA sample**. Select the finished DNA to retrieve it. The machine processes one sample at a time and preserves all nine saved slots.
5. Carry the **DNA Samples**, hold **Book of Life**, and press **RMB**. It consumes **every DNA sample in your carried inventory** and **all currently available mana** to awaken the corresponding companions. You need some mana to begin.

Clear space around you for the creatures. Ground creatures need dry support, flyers need clear air, and aquatic creatures need nearby water. If any sample cannot be summoned safely or its source mod is unavailable, the entire batch keeps its DNA and mana.

## Bloodbound companions

Companions retain their source's appearance, size, attachment choices, movement type and maximum health. They follow you and assist against creatures you attack, creatures that attack you, or attackers of your companions. They use a **ten-damage bloodbound projectile every 1.5 seconds**, with a thirty-block firing range and clear sight. Owner and allied companions are protected from their bolts; normal server PvP rules apply.

They are **mortal allies**. They have no automatic expiry and **survive world saves and restarts**, including their owner and species identity. They remain in their summon world while you are absent and resume following when you return nearby. Their original hostile abilities and death loot are replaced by bloodbound combat. Bloodbound copies cannot be sampled again.

The cloak provides concealment rather than armor protection. Environmental damage and other ordinary damage sources can still hurt and reveal you. Equip or remove it to enable or disable concealment.

## Crafting

Craft **Bloody Book** and **Book of Life** at **Arcanist's Workbench → Spellblades**. The **Vitae Extractor** stays at a **tier-two normal Workbench → Crafting**. Each recipe takes five seconds.

| Item | Ingredients |
| --- | --- |
| Bloody Book | 8 Linen Scraps, 4 Light Leather, 3 Arcane Crystal Shards, 2 Essence of Life, 1 Evil Core |
| Book of Life | 8 Linen Scraps, 4 Light Leather, 3 Arcane Crystal Shards, 5 Essence of Life, 1 Evil Core |
| Vitae Extractor | 6 Iron Bars, 4 Copper Bars, 8 Stone, 2 Arcane Crystal Shards |

Blood droplets come from extraction; DNA comes from the machine. Their tooltips identify the saved species. The extractor saves all contents and uses ordinary container break handling. It performs no work while unloaded; unfinished inputs remain intact.

Previously placed extractors also work with the brewing screen. Closing it lets processing continue while the machine remains loaded. DNA replaces the original blood slot, so a full extractor still has output room. Missing essence, invalid species data or blocked output is explained on screen without spending the ingredients. Use the Storage, Backpack and Hotbar tabs to select carried ingredients or retrieve DNA.

## Blood Furnace

The Blood Furnace and Vitae Extractor screens close with **Space**, **Tab** or
**Escape**. The furnace processing area shows its progress bar; blocked-work
feedback appears in the footer.

The **Blood Furnace** smelts and cooks **one recipe at a time at twice normal speed**. One **Blood Droplet** fuels **five minutes of active processing**. It has one blood slot, four input slots and four output slots, and uses the available native smelting and cooking recipes.

Leave a clear **2×2 footprint** for the masonry hearth and blood reservoir. Existing furnaces may need to be collected and placed again to reserve the larger space.

Place it and use **Interact**. Select ingredients from **Storage, Backpack or Hotbar**: **LMB transfers a stack; RMB transfers one**. Blood enters the fuel slot automatically. Select a machine slot to collect its contents, and use **Pause/Resume** to control work. Full outputs or missing ingredients stop fuel use. Closing the screen lets it continue while loaded; saves and restarts retain its contents, remaining fuel and current progress. It does no work while unloaded.

Craft at **tier-two Workbench → Crafting**, **12 seconds**: **12 Iron Bars, 6 Copper Bars, 4 Revel Bars, 2 Aequalis Bars, 6 Arcane Matter, 16 Stone and 1 Evil Core**.
