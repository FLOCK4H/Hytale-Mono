# Blood Magic

Use the Bloody Book to collect creature blood, the Book of Life to summon companions and the Leech Book to drain enemies and heal yourself. All three books work in your hand or through the Arcane Wand, including from a carried Spellbook Pouch.

Carrying **Bloody Book** drains **8 HP every 5 seconds**, including when it is inside a carried spellbook pouch. The curse stops when you remove it; its pulses do not interrupt blood extraction.

## Leech Book

Hold **RMB** while aiming at a living creature or player within **16 blocks**. The blood stream locks onto that target. Each full second deals **30 base damage**, restores **20 health** and costs **10 mana**. Armor and damage protection apply; a rejected hit gives no healing. Healing stops at your maximum health.

Keep holding RMB: damage, healing and mana spending begin after the first full second, then repeat each second until the channel ends.

A red beam connects you to the target, with droplets flowing toward you and pulses at both ends.

With Leech selected on the **Arcane Wand**, hold **RMB** standing or crouching. Carry the Leech Book loose or inside a carried Spellbook Pouch.

Release RMB, lose sight, move out of range or change your held item to stop. Death, an invalid target or insufficient mana also ends the channel. It does not switch to another target automatically. Player targets require **PvP** to be enabled.

Craft with exactly **one Rex Blood Sample, one Dreadmaw Blood Sample, one Yeti Blood Sample and one Emberwulf Blood Sample** at **Axo's Workbench → Library**, **12 seconds**. Extract each with the Bloody Book from a different living creature. These samples can also be processed into DNA or used as Blood Furnace fuel.

## From blood to a companion

1. Wear the full **Arcane Veil set** to reduce creature detection to five blocks. Stay outside that range while preparing an extraction. The separate **Invisibility Cloak** conceals you from creatures and players.
2. Hold **Bloody Book**, aim at a living creature within **eight blocks**, and press **RMB** once. Keep aiming and holding the book for **eight uninterrupted seconds**. Taking damage, changing items, losing sight or moving out of range interrupts extraction. It costs no mana.
3. Collect the **Blood Droplet**. Its tooltip names the creature, and its identity stays with the vial through storage, trading and restarts. **Each individual gives blood only once**, shared across players. A full inventory leaves the vial on the ground.
4. Place a **Vitae Extractor** and use **Interact** to open its brewing screen. Select blood and **Essence of Life** from the inventory below: **LMB transfers a stack; RMB transfers one**. The machine has an essence slot, three vial stations and five reserve slots. Extraction begins automatically and shows the species and progress. After **five loaded seconds**, **one droplet + one essence = one DNA sample**. Select the finished DNA to retrieve it. The machine processes one sample at a time and preserves all nine saved slots.
5. Carry the **DNA Samples**, hold **Book of Life**, and press **RMB**. It consumes **every DNA sample in your carried inventory** and **all currently available mana** to awaken the corresponding companions. You need some mana to begin.

Clear space around you for the creatures. Ground creatures need dry support, flyers need clear air, and aquatic creatures need nearby water. If any sample cannot be summoned safely or its source mod is unavailable, the entire batch keeps its DNA and mana.

## Bloodbound companions

Companions keep the sampled creature's appearance, size, attachments, movement type and maximum health. They follow you and assist against creatures you attack, creatures that attack you, or attackers of your companions. They use a **ten-damage bloodbound projectile every 1.5 seconds**, with a thirty-block firing range and clear sight. Owner and allied companions are protected from their bolts; normal server PvP rules apply.

They are **mortal allies**. They have no automatic expiry and **survive world saves and restarts**, including their owner and species identity. They remain in their summon world while you are absent and resume following when you return nearby. Their original hostile abilities and death loot are replaced by bloodbound combat. Bloodbound copies cannot be sampled again.

The cloak provides concealment rather than armor protection. Environmental damage and other ordinary damage sources can still hurt and reveal you. Equip or remove it to enable or disable concealment.

## Crafting

Craft **Bloody Book** and **Book of Life** at **Axo's Workbench → Library**. The **Vitae Extractor** is in **Axo's Workbench → Blood Magic**. Each recipe takes five seconds.

| Item | Ingredients |
| --- | --- |
| Bloody Book | 8 Linen Scraps, 4 Light Leather, 3 Arcane Crystal Shards, 2 Essence of Life, 1 Evil Core |
| Book of Life | 8 Linen Scraps, 4 Light Leather, 3 Arcane Crystal Shards, 5 Essence of Life, 1 Evil Core |
| Vitae Extractor | 6 Iron Bars, 4 Copper Bars, 8 Stone, 2 Arcane Crystal Shards |
| Frozen Chest | 6 Iron Bars, 4 Copper Bars, 8 Ice, 2 Arcane Crystal Shards, 2 Ice Essence |

Blood droplets come from extraction; DNA comes from the machine. Their tooltips identify the saved species. The extractor saves all contents and uses ordinary container break handling. It performs no work while unloaded; unfinished inputs remain intact.

Closing it lets processing continue while the machine remains loaded. DNA replaces the original blood slot, so a full extractor still has output room. Missing essence, invalid species data or blocked output is explained on screen without spending the ingredients. Use the Storage, Backpack and Hotbar tabs to select carried ingredients or retrieve DNA.

## Frozen Chest

![Frozen Chest](assets/images/catalog/Axo_Frozen_Chest.png)

Craft a **Frozen Chest** at **Axo's Workbench → Blood Magic** in **5 seconds**.
It stores **Blood Droplets and DNA Samples only**. A single chest has **4 rows**
of storage. Place another beside it, facing the same direction, to join them
into a full two-block chest with **8 rows**. Opening, moving items and breaking
the chest use the normal chest controls.

Keep blood and DNA in your **inventory**, a **Frozen Chest** or an **Astral
Satchel**. Ordinary chests and other storage reject them. The Vitae Extractor
still accepts blood for processing, and the Blood Furnace accepts blood in its
fuel slot. Collect finished DNA from the extractor; it does not accept DNA
deposits as extra storage. Stored species and source data survive transfers and
ordinary saves.

## Blood Furnace

The Blood Furnace and Vitae Extractor screens close with **Space**, **Tab** or
**Escape**. The furnace shows a progress bar and explains blocked processing at the bottom of its screen.

The **Blood Furnace** smelts and cooks **one recipe at a time at twice normal speed**. One **Blood Droplet** fuels **five minutes of active processing**. The small flask on its front shows the remaining fuel and drains while it works. Its level stays still while paused, out of ingredients or unable to fit the output. It has one blood slot, four input slots and four output slots, and uses the available native smelting and cooking recipes.

Leave a clear **2×2 footprint** for the masonry hearth and blood reservoir. Existing furnaces may need to be collected and placed again to reserve the larger space.

Place it and use **Interact**. Select ingredients from **Storage, Backpack or Hotbar**: **LMB transfers a stack; RMB transfers one**. Blood enters the fuel slot automatically. Select a machine slot to collect its contents, and use **Pause/Resume** to control work. Full outputs or missing ingredients stop fuel use. Closing the screen lets it continue while loaded; saves and restarts retain its contents, remaining fuel and current progress. It does no work while unloaded.

Craft at **Axo's Workbench → Blood Magic**, **12 seconds**: **12 Iron Bars, 6 Copper Bars, 4 Revel Bars, 2 Aequalis Bars, 6 Arcane Matter, 16 Stone and 1 Evil Core**.
