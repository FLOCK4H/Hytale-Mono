# Crafting

## Arcane Workbench (Axo Tales tab)

Used for Axo Tales gear and spellbooks.

### Sa’r gear

- **Sa’r Boots**: Dark Feathers ×4 + Cobalt Bars ×4 + Arcane Crystal Shards ×6 + Arcane Matter ×12
- **Sa’r Diadem**: Arcane Crystal Shards ×4 + Cobalt Bars ×2 + Arcane Matter ×8
- **Sa’r Armor**: Cobalt Bars ×12 + Arcane Matter ×20 + Arcane Crystal Shards ×10
- **Sa’r Warfists**: Azure Kelp ×2 + Arcane Matter ×2 + Arcane Crystal Shards ×2

### Weapons

- **Axo’s Ancient Sword**: Arcane Matter ×20 + Arcane Crystal Shards ×15 + Essence of Fire ×12 + Essence of Ice ×12

### Spellbooks

Each crafted spellbook recipe includes:
- Arcane Crystal Shards ×2
- Arcane Matter ×2

Plus one extra ingredient:
- **Healing Book**: Azure Fruit ×4
- **Teleport Book**: Blue Crystal Shards ×2
- **Mining Book**: Volcanic Cobble ×10
- **Immunity Book**: Cobalt Shield ×1
- **Horde Book**: Feran Candle ×3
- **Book of Doom**: Voidhearts ×4
- **Morph Book**: Dark Feathers ×2
- **Taunt Book**: Essence of Fire ×5
- **Flame Book**: Essence of Fire ×20

Notes:
- Frost Book has no recipe by default (Rune Knight drop).
- Kudu Boots can be crafted, and can also drop.

## Alchemybench (vanilla)

### Axo’s Empty Potion Bottle

- Empty Potion Bottle (`Potion_Empty`) ×1
- Arcane Crystal Shard (`Ingredient_Crystal_Arcane`) ×1

### Potions

Every Axo Tales potion recipe requires:
- Axo’s Empty Potion Bottle ×1

Then add:
- **Swift Potion**: Arcane Crystal Shards ×2 + Cyan Hibiscus ×2
- **Strength Potion**: Arcane Crystal Shards ×2 + Adamantite Ingots ×10
- **Rabbit Potion**: Arcane Crystal Shards ×2 + Dark Feather ×1
- **Invisibility Potion**: Arcane Crystal Shards ×2 + Azure Kelp ×1
- **Curse Potion**: Arcane Crystal Shards ×2 + Essence of Fire ×5
