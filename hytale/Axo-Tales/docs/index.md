## Explore Axo Tales

<div class="card-grid card-grid--feature">
  <div class="card" data-reveal>
    <h3>Spellbooks have clear roles</h3>
    <p>Healing, teleport, mining, immunity, horde, morph, frost, flame, taunt, doom, and light all have distinct cast timing and a distinct job. </p>
  </div>
  <div class="card" data-reveal>
    <h3>Mana lives on gear</h3>
    <p>Axo Tales armor pieces add max mana and mana regeneration, then split into real specialties: movement boots, aquatic diadem, heavy chest, projectile warfists, stealth cloak, and a spellblade sword that still rewards carrying it.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card" data-reveal>
    <h3>The world feeds progression</h3>
    <p>Arcane Matter ore, Arcane Crystals, and creature drops all push you back into recipes. Cloud and Bounce Blocks then turn spare loot into traversal tools instead of dead-end collectibles.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card" data-reveal>
    <h3>Encounters feed your equipment</h3>
    <p>Cursed Knights are worth farming for materials and drops, while Elder Mages give the mod a companion lane that ties directly into crystal routing and combat support.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Quick install path

<div class="card-grid card-grid--steps">
  <div class="card" data-reveal>
    <span class="step-tag">1</span>
    <h3>Build or download the current jar</h3>
    <p>Use an Axo Tales jar built for Hytale <code>0.7.3</code>.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card" data-reveal>
    <span class="step-tag">2</span>
    <h3>Install it to the shared mods folder</h3>
    <p>Put it in <code>C:\Users\&lt;you&gt;\AppData\Roaming\Hytale\UserData\Mods</code>.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card" data-reveal>
    <span class="step-tag">3</span>
    <h3>Keep one copy and restart if Windows says the jar is locked</h3>
    <p>Old jars in that folder are the fastest way to test the wrong build by accident.</p>
  </div>
</div>
