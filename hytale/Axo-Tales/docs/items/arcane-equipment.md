# Wand and Spellbook Pouch

Use **Axo's Arcane Wand** to cast books in your inventory or a carried pouch. You still need to own each spellbook, and its normal mana cost and cooldown still apply. All spellbooks also work directly from your hand.

All **seventeen** books are supported, including Lightning, Meteorain, [Bloody Book, Leech Book and Book of Life](../blood-magic.md). Storms preserve their 80-mana cost, 40-block radius, eight-second duration and shared cooldown through the wand. The wand also preserves the eight-second extraction and Book of Life's entire-current-mana cost. DNA samples stay in ordinary carried inventory; the pouch stores books.

## Crafting

The wand is **Epic**, with the gold description title **Voice of the Elder Grove**. The pouch is Rare. Both take 5 seconds to craft at the Arcanist's Workbench, in the **Spellblades** tab, using mid-progression materials.

| Item | Ingredients |
| --- | --- |
| Axo's Arcane Wand | 6 Oak Trunks, 4 Cobalt Bars, 8 Arcane Matter, 4 Arcane Crystal Shards, 6 Agretis Bars, 1 Evil Core |
| Axo's Spellbook Pouch | 8 Medium Leather, 8 Linen Scraps, 2 Cobalt Bars, 4 Arcane Matter, 2 Arcane Crystal Shards, 4 Agretis Bars |

## Using the pouch

Keep the pouch in your hotbar, storage, backpack or utility/tool inventory. It holds **18 spellbooks**, including duplicate books. It rejects other items, wands and nested pouches.

Newly picked-up spellbooks go into an available carried pouch automatically, including when your ordinary inventory is full. If your pouches are full, normal inventory pickup takes over; if everything is full, the book stays on the ground. Books manually taken out of the pouch stay outside it so you can cast by hand.

Hold the pouch and **right-click** to open its inventory. You can move existing books into it manually. Books stay inside the physical pouch when you save, reconnect, drop it or trade it. Different pouches have separate contents.

## Wand controls

| Control | Action |
| --- | --- |
| LMB | Open the radial spell wheel |
| RMB | Cast the selected spell |
| Crouch + RMB | Use the selected spell's alternate action |
| RMB, Space or Esc, with the wheel open | Close the wheel |

Bind crouch to Shift in your Hytale controls to use **Shift + RMB** for alternate actions. The action follows your crouch binding.

The translucent spell wheel shows the supported books around a transparent center. Hover for the spell's actions and server-configured mana cost; click its icon to equip it, or use **RMB, Space or Esc** to close. Clicking the backdrop also closes it. Loose books and books in carried pouches unlock their spells; a pouch is optional for wand casting. Missing books are disabled. The selected spell is saved on the wand across reconnects, and every selection rechecks the held wand and carried books on the server.

With the wand, crouch+RMB fires Healing's bolt, resets Morph's form or cycles Mining's tunnel shape. Hold ordinary RMB to charge Mining. Other spells have no alternate action. Direct hand-held books retain their existing LMB actions. Losing the last carried copy of the needed book prevents further casts, including casts still waiting for their release delay.

For **Leech Book**, aim at a living target within **16 blocks** and hold **RMB**, standing or crouching. Keep its book in carried inventory or a carried pouch. Keep holding RMB to maintain the blood stream. Each full second deals **30 base damage**, restores up to **20 health** and spends **10 mana**. Release RMB to stop the drain.

The wand starts with Silver selected. If that book is unavailable, right-clicking opens selection instead. Use the free Silver starter for your first pouch spell, or store another book and select it.
