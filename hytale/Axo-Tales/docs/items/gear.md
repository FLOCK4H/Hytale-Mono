# Gear and Spellblade

<div class="page-header">
  <p class="eyebrow">Armor and weapon route</p>
  <p class="lead-text">Axo Tales gear does more than pad stats. The Sa'r set builds mana and stamina, Kudu Boots change how water works, the cloak leans into stealth, and the Ancient Sword keeps one foot in melee and one foot in spellcasting.</p>
</div>

## Shared Sa'r baseline

<div class="accent-card" data-reveal>
  <h3>Every Sa'r piece now carries real value</h3>
  <p>Each Sa'r armor item grants <strong>+25 max mana</strong> and <strong>+2 mana every 2 seconds</strong>, then layers on the set baseline: <strong>+10 stamina</strong>, <strong>+25% stamina regeneration</strong>, and <strong>+16% defense</strong>. All current armor pieces also use the longer-life durability pass: <code>MaxDurability=500</code> and <code>DurabilityLossOnHit=0.5</code>.</p>
</div>

## Numbers worth remembering

- Ancient Sword primary swings: 83 physical base damage.
- Ancient Sword charged stab: 105 physical base damage with a longer forward dash.
- Ancient Slash projectile: 50 damage, 20 mana, 1.25s cooldown, 0.34s cast delay by default.
- Warfists projectile: 25 damage and 2-second stun while worn and unarmed.
