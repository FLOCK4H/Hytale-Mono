## Arcane Veil robe

![Arcane Veil Hood](../assets/images/catalog/Axo_Arcane_Veil_Hood.png)

![Arcane Veil Robe](../assets/images/catalog/Axo_Arcane_Veil_Robe.png)

![Arcane Veil Leggings](../assets/images/catalog/Axo_Arcane_Veil_Leggings.png)

Three Rare pieces: **Arcane Veil Hood**, **Arcane Veil Robe**, **Arcane Veil Leggings**. Wear all three for these totals: **+30% movement speed, +25% maximum health, mana and stamina, +5 mana per second and 20% incoming damage absorption**. The light cloth pieces have minimal armor; hands remain free. Equipping the set increases capacity without refilling current stats. The complete Veil set also gives **+50% crafting speed**.

Wearing the full set once **permanently unlocks one nine-slot Backpack row**, without needing a sword. Open **Inventory → Backpack**. Full **Sa'r** unlocks **18 bonus slots**; full **Ancient** unlocks **27**. Standard backpack scrolls retain their original upgrades. Other robe bonuses end on unequip. Older occupied overflow rows move safely into the backpack; the main inventory keeps its normal layout. Existing backpack items and other mods' storage sizes are preserved.

Native hostile NPCs outside **5 blocks** are suppressed; Axo Cursed Knights also check this range when choosing/firing at targets. Other mods' independent creature behavior can bypass native attitudes. This is stealth from NPCs rather than invisibility from players.

Craft each piece at **Axo's Workbench → Armory**, 6 seconds:

| Piece | Ingredients |
| --- | --- |
| Hood | 10 Linen Scraps, 5 Arcane Matter, 4 Arcane Crystal Shards, 4 Agretis Bar, 3 Revel Bar, 1 Evil Core. |
| Robe | 16 Linen Scraps, 4 Medium Leather, 8 Arcane Matter, 6 Arcane Crystal Shards, 8 Agretis Bar, 1 Evil Core. |
| Leggings | 12 Linen Scraps, 3 Medium Leather, 6 Arcane Matter, 4 Arcane Crystal Shards, 6 Agretis Bar, 1 Evil Core. |

For the complete Agretis/Revel/Aequalis bar costs, see [New Metals](../materials.md). Each crafted gear piece also requires one Evil Core.

## Shared Sa'r bonuses {#shared-sar-baseline}

Wear all four pieces once to permanently earn **18 bonus Backpack slots**. It stays unlocked after changing armor, without needing a sword. Standard backpack scrolls add their normal capacity on top.

All four Sa'r pieces grant a further **+35% movement speed, +30% maximum health and mana, and 12.5% damage absorption** when worn together. The complete set also gives **+100% crafting speed**. These bonuses stack with those on each piece. The set has navy-steel plates, violet cloth, gold fittings and teal crystals. Its open diadem leaves your hair visible.

**Most crafted equipment and spellbooks use Evil Cores from Cursed Knights.** The complete recipes below include them; Ancient gear has higher costs.

**Ancient Helm** also grants **+30% stamina regeneration**, underwater breathing, **+50% swimming speed** and **10 mana every four seconds**. Incoming projectiles ricochet toward their attacker with **1.3× base damage**. Returned projectiles use native collision and can be dodged or stopped by terrain; they cannot ricochet again.

**Ancient Chestplate** also grants **+20% maximum health**, **+50% defense**, **+30% stamina regeneration** and **+30% melee/projectile damage**. Hostile native NPCs detect the wearer only within **four blocks**. **Crouch + Jump** toggles flight with **ten seconds of fuel**. The flight bar shows remaining time; switching flight off pauses consumption, and landing refills it. Removing the chestplate ends flight; re-equipping it in mid-air does not refill the tank.

**Ancient Gauntlets** also grant **+20% defense** and **+30% stamina regeneration**. Select an **empty hotbar slot** and press **LMB** while aiming clear of nearby blocks and entities to throw a large blue fireball. A **0.32-second wind-up** leads into the throw, followed by a return to rest. Looking at a nearby block or entity preserves ordinary LMB actions. The fireball explodes on entity or terrain contact within a **four-block radius**, dealing one **71-110 base damage** roll to each affected target. It has a **0.9-second cooldown** and does no block damage.

**Ancient Boots** also grant **+20% defense**, **+30% movement speed**, **three blocks of extra jump height**, **one extra air jump** and fall-damage immunity. Release Jump between jumps. Durability is **900** per piece, or **1,200** for the chestplate.

Wear all four Ancient pieces for these **additional** bonuses:

- **+400 maximum health** as a flat addition after the chestplate's health scaling, and **+100 maximum stamina**.

- **+50% movement speed** and **+15% attack damage** across melee, projectiles and spells.

- **40% resistance to every elemental damage type** and **50% additional defense** against physical/projectile damage.

- **+50% maximum mana**, **10 mana every two seconds**, and **+50% stamina regeneration**.

- **One additional air jump**, giving **two air jumps** with the boots.

- Permanently unlock **27 bonus Backpack slots**. Existing contents and ordinary backpack upgrades remain intact.

The complete Ancient set also gives **+300% crafting speed**.

The set bonus stacks with piece bonuses: boots and full set give **+95% movement speed** together; the chestplate and full set give **+49.5% melee/projectile damage**. Defense reduces damage remaining after ordinary armor. Mixing Ancient and Sa'r pieces keeps individual bonuses but grants neither complete-set bonus. Capacity refreshes do not refill current health, mana or stamina.

See [the complete recipes](../crafting.md#ancient-armor). Ancient armor is a separate progression path from Sa'r equipment.

## Arcane Pickaxe

![Arcane Pickaxe](../assets/images/catalog/Axo_Arcane_Pickaxe.png)

This **Epic** pickaxe has **1200 durability**. **LMB** excavates a **3×3×3 volume** into the targeted surface, within six blocks. **Hold RMB** to keep mining. Each successful excavation costs **10 mana and one durability**. Broken blocks release normal mining drops. Bedrock, containers, workbenches and protected blocks are skipped.

Craft at **Axo's Workbench → Items**, **12 seconds**: **12 Revel Bars, 8 Aequalis Bars, 8 Arcane Matter, 16 Arcane Crystal Shards, 4 Heavy Leather and 2 Evil Cores**.

## Ancient Pickaxe

![Ancient Pickaxe](../assets/images/catalog/Axo_Ancient_Pickaxe.png)

An **unbreakable Legendary pickaxe**. Ore blocks give **three times their normal loot**, including Axo Tales ores. Ordinary stone and dirt keep their usual drops. **LMB** mines one block and can hit nearby enemies for **24 base physical damage**, with strong outward knockback. **Hold RMB** for continuous **3×3×3 mining**, without a mana cost. On clear ground, a use gives you a short backward hop with braking. Further uses while airborne add no recoil. Walls can stop the movement.

Craft at **Axo's Workbench → Items**, **24 seconds**: **1 Arcane Pickaxe, 64 Adamantite Ingots, 96 Aequalis Bars, 160 Arcane Matter, 128 Arcane Crystal Shards, 24 Voidhearts, 12 Evil Cores and 2 Mithril Ore**. Fossor Souls can produce the Mithril. Feeding them Arcane Crystals increases its chance.

### Ground strike, both pickaxes

While standing on the ground, press **F** with either pickaxe. Aimed-at chests, workbenches and other Interact actions take priority. Take the tool in both hands, raise it overhead and strike after **1.5 seconds**. The impact mines a **3×3 area, 2 blocks deep**, then launches you about **25 blocks upward** in clear space. Protection and terrain checks still apply. It will not launch if no block can be broken.

**Space** cancels during the **2.2-second animation**, including the launch during recovery. Switching tools, dying or canceling the action also stops it. You can start another ground strike after **3 seconds**. F follows your normal Interact binding if you have changed it.

## Arcane Sword damage and controls {#numbers-worth-remembering}

- Arcane Sword controls: LMB uses native longsword attacks/charged stab; RMB casts Arcane Slash.

- Arcane Sword primary swings: 83 physical base damage.

- Arcane Sword charged stab: 105 physical base damage with a longer forward dash.

- Arcane Sword primary attacks/charges run 20% faster, with matching animation playback.

- Normal and charged melee hits push enemies outward, tuned for roughly **3-4 blocks** on open ground; obstacles and knockback resistance affect travel.

- Arcane Slash projectile: 50 damage, 20 mana, 1.25s cooldown, 0.34s cast delay by default.

- Warfists projectile: 25 damage and 2-second stun while worn and unarmed.

## Ancient Sword

![Ancient Sword](../assets/images/catalog/Axo_Ancient_Sword.png)

A **Legendary** greatsword with a silver blade and amethyst channel. **LMB** retains the native longsword combo: **160 base damage** on ordinary swings and **200** on the charged stab. **RMB** releases a **100-damage slash** after a **0.34-second wind-up**, for **30 mana**, with a **1.25-second cooldown**. Durability: **1,500**.

The Arcane Sword is consumed as an ingredient. Existing Arcane Swords remain the Epic item until crafted into this separate weapon.

## Mana with other mods

Axo gear adds its bonuses to Hytale's native Mana stat. Other mods' native mana capacity, modifiers and regeneration can coexist with these bonuses; taking Axo gear off does not empty their pool. Mods that implement a separate private mana resource need their own integration.

## Bloodfang Daggers

Epic dual daggers with curved silver blades, gold guards, indigo grips and amethyst seals. **Grounded LMB attacks** add a short forward dash, at most once every **0.4 seconds**. The dash cannot stack and slows equally on the ground and in the air. Jumping just after LMB keeps the ordinary dagger combo without triggering a pounce launch. Basic hits add a small outward knockback. **30 physical damage per basic hit**, **100 total across the full signature** (30 + 30 + 40), with **60% faster attack cadence than native daggers**. RMB guards; the native signature requires its normal energy charge. Damage remains subject to armor/protection.

Hold **LMB for 0.8 seconds**, then release to throw both daggers toward the target's head. Each blade deals **125 base damage**, for **250** if both hit. The throw has a **5-second cooldown**; ordinary taps remain available. The blades leave your hands when thrown and return during recovery.

Craft at **Axo's Workbench → Armory**, 12 seconds: 24 Arcane Matter, 16 Arcane Crystal Shards, 10 Heavy Leather, 16 Revel Bars, 12 Aequalis Bars and 1 Evil Core.

## Rimefang

![Rimefang sword](../assets/images/catalog/Axo_Rimefang.png)

A Rare steel sword with a cyan blade groove, silver guard and navy grip. It uses native sword combos, RMB guard and the sword signature. Normal attacks deal **11 / 14 / 21 / 32 base physical damage**, matching cobalt sword strength.

Successful, unblocked hits freeze the enemy for **2 seconds**, stopping movement and attacks. More hits refresh the two-second timer. Blocked and cancelled hits do not apply freeze.

Craft at **Axo's Workbench → Armory**: **8 Cobalt Bars, 6 Ice Essence, 4 Arcane Matter, 3 Medium Leather, 10 Revel Bars and 1 Evil Core**; 6 seconds. Durability: 150.

Arcane Veil Leggings cover your footwear while equipped, following native leggings behavior.

Crafting speed bonuses apply while wearing the complete matching set and speed up your personal crafting queue: Veil **1.5×**, Sa'r **2×**, Ancient **4×**. Partial or mixed sets do not give a crafting bonus.
