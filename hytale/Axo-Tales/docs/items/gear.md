# Gear

## Sa’r Boots

**What it does**
- While worn: increases max mana and regenerates mana.
- Extra effects while worn: faster movement, higher jumps, doubled stamina, and fall damage immunity.

**Crafting (Arcane Workbench → Axo Tales)**
- Dark Feathers ×4
- Cobalt Bars ×4
- Arcane Crystal Shards ×6
- Arcane Matter ×12

## Invisibility Cloak

**What it does**
- While worn: keeps you invisible and dulls nearby NPC aggression.
- While worn: increases max mana.

**Crafting**
- No recipe by default.

## Kudu Boots

**What it does**
- While worn: turns water beneath you into snow brick so you can walk across.
- While worn: increases max mana.

**How to get**
- Craft (Arcane Workbench), or
- Drop from the Kudu Rune Knight (chance is configurable).

**Crafting (Arcane Workbench → Axo Tales)**
- Arcane Crystal Shards ×2
- Arcane Matter ×2
- Essence of Fire (`Ingredient_Fire_Essence`) ×20

## Axo’s Ancient Sword

**What it does**
- While carried in inventory: increases max mana.
- RMB: casts an Ancient Slash projectile (mana cost + cooldown are configurable).

**Crafting (Arcane Workbench → Axo Tales)**
- Arcane Matter ×20
- Arcane Crystal Shards ×15
- Essence of Fire (`Ingredient_Fire_Essence`) ×12
- Essence of Ice (`Ingredient_Ice_Essence`) ×12
