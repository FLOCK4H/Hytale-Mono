# Items (Overview)

This page lists every Axo Tales item and how to get it. Details are on the category pages.

## Gear

- **Sa’r Boots**: Arcane Workbench craft. Mana bonuses + regen while worn; extra movement/jump/stamina and fall immunity. See [Gear](gear.md).
- **Sa’r Diadem**: Arcane Workbench craft. Mana bonuses + regen while worn. See [Gear](gear.md).
- **Sa’r Armor**: Arcane Workbench craft. Mana bonuses + regen while worn. See [Gear](gear.md).
- **Sa’r Warfists**: Arcane Workbench craft. Mana bonuses + regen while worn. See [Gear](gear.md).
- **Invisibility Cloak**: no recipe by default (spawn/creative). While worn, keeps you invisible and dulls nearby NPC aggression.
- **Kudu Boots**: Arcane Workbench craft (and can also drop from the Kudu Rune Knight). Walk on water; increases max mana.
- **Axo’s Ancient Sword**: Arcane Workbench craft. RMB casts an Ancient Slash (mana cost); carrying it increases max mana.

## Spellbooks

All spellbooks are used with mouse buttons:
- **RMB**: cast (most books)
- **LMB**: extra action on a few books (Healing / Morph)

See [Spellbooks](spellbooks.md) for exact behavior, mana costs, and recipes.

- **Axo’s Healing Book**: Arcane Workbench craft.
- **Axo’s Teleport Book**: Arcane Workbench craft.
- **Axo’s Mining Book**: Arcane Workbench craft.
- **Axo’s Immunity Book**: Arcane Workbench craft.
- **Axo’s Taunt Book**: Arcane Workbench craft.
- **Axo’s Horde Book**: Arcane Workbench craft.
- **Axo’s Book of Doom**: Arcane Workbench craft.
- **Axo’s Morph Book**: Arcane Workbench craft.
- **Axo’s Flame Book**: Arcane Workbench craft.
- **Axo’s Frost Book**: no recipe by default (drops from the Kudu Rune Knight).

## Potions

All Axo Tales potions require **Axo’s Empty Potion Bottle** as an ingredient. See [Potions](potions.md).

- **Axo’s Empty Potion Bottle**: Alchemybench craft (from a vanilla empty bottle + Arcane Crystal Shard).
- **Swift Potion**: Alchemybench craft.
- **Strength Potion**: Alchemybench craft.
- **Rabbit Potion**: Alchemybench craft.
- **Invisibility Potion**: Alchemybench craft.
- **Curse Potion**: Alchemybench craft (throwable splash vial).

## Blocks / Ores / Materials

See [Blocks & Ores](../blocks-ores.md).

- **Arcane Matter**: mine Arcane Matter Ore.
- **Arcane Matter Ore (Stone)**: generates in rock; drops Arcane Matter + stone cobble.
- **Arcane Matter Ore (Volcanic)**: generates in volcanic rock; drops Arcane Matter + volcanic cobble.
- **Arcane Grass**: creative/spawn (breaks into dirt).
- **Custom Placeholder Block**: debug marker (creative/spawn).
