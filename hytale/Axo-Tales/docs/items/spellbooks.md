  <p class="eyebrow">Book lineup</p>
  <p class="lead-text">Cast spellbooks with <strong>RMB</strong>. Choose healing, utility, mining, summoning, blood magic, storms or terrain control to suit your build.</p>

  <p>Craft advanced spellbooks at the Arcanist's Workbench <strong>Spellblades</strong> tab. Hold RMB to charge Mining and use LMB to cycle its shape.</p>

Frost Book requires **4 Revel Bars**; Doom Book requires **4 Aequalis Bars**, alongside the other ingredients and Evil Core. See [New Metals](../materials.md).

Terrain-changing spells preserve chests, workbenches and other blocks with saved state. Frost and Flame leave these blocks intact; Mining and Taunt skip them too.

## Cast from a wand or by hand

Every book below can also be cast through the [Arcane Wand](arcane-equipment.md) while it is stored in a carried Spellbook Pouch. Mana costs and alternate actions are unchanged.

**Axo's Lightning Book** and **Axo's Meteorain Book** call a storm at visible terrain under your aim within 40 blocks; aiming into the sky centers it on you. Each affects a **40-block radius**, dealing **50 damage every second for eight seconds** (400 before armor/protection). The caster is spared. Lightning has animated cyan bolts and runes; Meteorain rains molten stones with fire trails and impact bursts. The storm remains where it was cast.

Both use **80 mana**, a **0.45-second invocation** and a **shared 24-second cooldown**. Keep the book or selected wand in the same slot until release. Wand and Spellbook Pouch support both. Dreadmaw has an **independent 10% drop chance for each book**; both can drop together.

Meteorain's molten rocks descend from **60 blocks overhead**, with longer fire trails and timed impact bursts. This high-sky presentation does not change its damage area or eight-second duration.

## Blood Magic

## Casting tips

- Healing, Morph, and Mining all use LMB for an extra function, so they play differently from the standard pure-RMB books.
- Light Book is utility-only. It is there to light space and travel with you, not to deal damage.
- Let the cast finish before switching items. Teleport and Taunt use a short wind-up.
