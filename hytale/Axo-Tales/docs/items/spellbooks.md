# Spellbooks

## Common rules

- Most spellbooks cast on **RMB**.
- Mana costs are configurable in `server-config.json`.
- Spellbooks and most gear are crafted at the **Arcane Workbench** (Axo Tales tab).

## Axo's Healing Book

- **LMB**: fires a Healing Bolt that fully heals what it hits.
- **RMB**: heals you; costs mana.
- Craft: Arcane Crystal Shards x2 + Arcane Matter x2 + Azure Fruit x4
- Config: `healingBook.*`

## Axo's Teleport Book

- **RMB**: teleports you to the block you're looking at; costs mana.
- Craft: Arcane Crystal Shards x2 + Arcane Matter x2 + Blue Crystal Shards x2
- Config: `teleportBook.*`

## Axo's Mining Book

- **RMB**: mines a shaped patch around the targeted block; costs mana.
- Craft: Arcane Crystal Shards x2 + Arcane Matter x2 + Volcanic Cobble x10
- Config: `miningBook.*`

## Axo's Immunity Book

- **RMB**: grants brief damage immunity; costs mana.
- Craft: Arcane Crystal Shards x2 + Arcane Matter x2 + Cobalt Shield x1
- Config: `immunityBook.*`

## Axo's Taunt Book

- **RMB**: launches you upward; on landing, deals area damage; costs mana.
- Craft: Arcane Crystal Shards x2 + Arcane Matter x2 + Essence of Fire x5
- Config: `tauntBook.*`

## Axo's Horde Book

- **RMB**: summons a short-lived brute horde that fights for you; costs mana.
- Craft: Arcane Crystal Shards x2 + Arcane Matter x2 + Feran Candle x3
- Config: `hordeBook.*`

## Axo's Book of Doom

- **RMB**: hurls a Doom Ball projectile; costs mana.
- Craft: Arcane Crystal Shards x2 + Arcane Matter x2 + Voidhearts x4
- Config: `doomBook.*`

## Axo's Morph Book

- **RMB**: fires a morph vortex to steal a target's form; costs mana.
- **LMB**: restores your own form.
- Craft: Arcane Crystal Shards x2 + Arcane Matter x2 + Dark Feathers x2
- Config: `morphBook.*`

## Axo's Flame Book

- **RMB**: launches a flame bolt that burns foes and turns terrain to volcanic rock; costs mana.
- Craft: Arcane Crystal Shards x2 + Arcane Matter x2 + Essence of Fire x20
- Config: `flameBook.*`

## Axo's Frost Book

- **RMB**: launches a frost shard that slows foes and turns terrain to snow brick; costs mana.
- Craft: no recipe by default.
- How to get: drop from the Kudu Rune Knight (chance is configurable).
- Config: `frostBook.*`
