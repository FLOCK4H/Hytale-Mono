# Runes and the Skill Tree

Craft a **Skill Tree Seed** at the **Arcanebench**, in **Spellblades**, using
**2 Life Essence and 1 Purple Orchid**. Plant it in open ground with room for a
large tree. It passes through five growth stages and matures after **15 real
minutes**. Its saved growth clock continues while the area is unloaded.

Interact with the fully grown trunk to open **Arbor Arcanum**. Select a skill to
see its gifts and prerequisite, then choose **Learn skill**. Close or Escape
returns to the world. Breaking the tree returns its seed; planting it again
starts a new growth cycle. Your learned skills remain yours.

The lower-left bonus summary shows every skill category with an icon, its total
percentage or amount, and the appropriate units. Locked special gifts are marked
until learned. Health, stamina, regeneration and reach bonuses add together.

## Collecting runes

Violet symbols appear on grass, beside tree trunks and on exposed ore. They are
medium-rare. New suitable chunks have a 50% surface-rune chance and a separate
25% attached-rune chance; terrain can prevent either placement. Previously dense
placements are thinned once as their chunks load. Collected runes do not respawn.
Break a symbol to release a glowing, harmless rune projectile toward the
**nearest living player**. It follows that player and gives **one rune** directly
to their skill balance when it reaches their body. Nothing needs to fit in their
inventory. Dead players and spectators cannot be selected.

There are 24 symbols, from **Alfa** through **Omega**. Every symbol is worth one
rune. A saved chunk is seeded once, so leaving and returning does not restore
collected runes. Your balance, skills and Lazarus cooldown survive ordinary
saves, reconnects, death and travel between dimensions of the same save.

## Skills

Every value below is an **additional bonus**. For example, Vis and Acies together
give **+7.5% attack damage**. You must learn the preceding skill on a branch;
having enough currency alone cannot skip it. The whole tree costs **220 runes**.

Full attack, defense and health branches each add **37.5%**. Full movement adds
**17.5% speed**. The attack branch adds **3 blocks of melee reach**. The health
branch adds **25 flat health** and regenerates **4 health every 2 seconds**.
Projectile resistance totals **35%** and applies in addition to ordinary defense.
Equipment and other native bonuses continue to compose with learned skills.

## Lazarus status

Learning Lazarus adds its violet Rho symbol beside the hotbar. **Ready** means it
can save you. **Immune** marks the three-second protection window, then a
**minutes:seconds** countdown shows when it will be ready again. Native protected
or already-cancelled hits do not consume it. Command and out-of-world damage
retain their native behavior.
